Le Village: boulangerie (v2, with its shop and its bake room, le fournil), café (with its salle and its terrasse), église (with the church and its parish hall, la salle paroissiale), mairie (with its hall and its post counter, la poste) and école (with its classroom and its math classroom, la classe de maths)
- All pictures (village map, the ten rooms, their close-ups, word sprites, blank sign plaques) were AI-generated with the Grok Bot built-in image generator, then cut out and composited by script. The alphabet letter cards on the école notice board were drawn onto the generated board by script.
- The words "La boulangerie", "Le café", "L'église", "La mairie" and "L'école" on the signs, "Le fournil", "La terrasse", "La poste", "La classe de maths" and "La salle paroissiale" on the small door plaques, and the letters on the école notice board, were drawn into the images with the font Lilita One (Juan Montoreano, SIL Open Font License 1.1). No font files are shipped with the site.
- Glosses, memory hooks and card text were written for this project. Example sentences come from the project's own word list (data/vocab.jsonl); the éclair, carte, serviette, croissant, orgue and carte d'identité examples, all the école examples, all the fournil examples, all the terrasse examples, all the poste examples, all the classe de maths examples and all the salle paroissiale examples, were written for this project, and the choisir example is adapted from it.
- Pronunciation audio (including the numbers board on le boulier) uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (French voice). No third-party code or services are used.

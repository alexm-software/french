## Checked and found clean
- Article, gender and room side (la = left, le = right) agree for all 4,313 nouns.
- In the live rooms: gender, article, side, plural, verb beret color and IPA are consistent for all 183 words.
- No two words share a hook any more (`devoir` verb/noun and `solde` m/f were the only pairs, and both are now split).
- Every example sentence contains its word; the only misses were irregular forms such as *je vais* and *yeux*.

## "Wall text" hooks: 88 given real mnemonics
Approved case by case: the misfiled words (e.g. *cabane*, *tribu*, *tension*, *incendie*), the political vocabulary with neutral scenes, and the milder crime, conflict and social words (e.g. *trahir*, *cambriolage*, *prisonnier*, *pauvreté*, *maladie*). The hooks avoid gore and take no political side. Six glosses were tidied along the way: `fléau`, `sultan`, `brigand`, `militant`, `détention`, `délinquant`.
Kept as wall text by decision: *séduction*, *sensuel*, *sexy*, *viril*, *virilité*, *sordide*; places and peoples; double-meaning words (*bombe*, *pistolet*, *héroïne*, …); *tabac*, *pipe*, *cendrier*; and grave words (killing, torture, terrorism, slavery, serious illness). *trou* is unchanged. 96 wall-text hooks remain.

## Not changed: needs a decision
- **"Wall text" hooks (185, mostly B2–C1).** Sensitive words (crime, war, illness, politics) get a museum-label definition instead of a mnemonic, e.g. *violence*: "wall text: VIOLENCE, the use of physical force…". This looks deliberate, so it was left alone. A few contain errors: "like English 'arrestation'", *attentat* "like English 'attempt'", and *tribu* is in the family though it isn't sensitive. `pipe` and `cendrier` use a related "Text plaque only" display rule.
- **`scripts/build_data.py` fails** because `content/site.json` no longer exists. That predates these changes: the old pipeline was removed when Le Village replaced it.

# Moving words without emptying rooms

Read before moving, removing or painting back any word.

- **Moving a word = three edits.** Remove it from the live room and inpaint its old spot (prompt "edit minimally, remove only X, keep everything else"; paste back only the item's region and look at the result at 2x; repeat for ghost legs or plinth stubs), move it in the plan data, and redraw its hook and painting in the new room (hooks describe the room they are painted in). Prefer an unbuilt plan room over squeezing into a finished live room. Check the parent room's painted miniature of a zoom spot too, since it can still show the removed item. <!-- B222 L127 -->
- **Sparse floor.** Never strip a live room below about 6 words, or below its count at base, without painting something back in the same pack. Keep a running sparse-rooms list (room, count now and at base, what was taken, which plan-moved words could fill it). <!-- B230 -->
- **Count plan-only moves (removal accounting).** A move that lives only in the data takes the word off the live site until its target room is painted. Track every such word and every room left sparse; regenerate the plan-only report with a script after every pack (total, plus breakdowns by pack and by planned building, and whether each planned room is built yet; `tools/unbuilt.py`), report both counts to the user at each stopping point, and paint words back where they fit. <!-- B231 G151 -->
- **Paint-backs cut both lists.** Pick a plan-only word whose meaning fits a sparse room in the same building, or a built room that is the word's own planned room. Re-plan it there (room_id plus the zoom_spot the room's other words use), paint it, and keep its existing hook where you can. Never paint into rooms that other workers own or have just repainted. Check their packs' file lists first. <!-- B232 -->
- **Placement bands apply to paint-backs too.** Adjectives and adverbs go in the 30–70% centre band, feminine words on the left and masculine words on the right. Choose the empty spot with that in mind before painting. <!-- B234 -->
- **Fix stale alts while you're there.** When you paint into a room, re-read its whole alt text and remove figures that earlier moves took out. <!-- B236 -->
- **Parent miniature and zoom hint.** When a zoom room is repainted, the parent room's miniature (the window, hatch, shelf or booth that opens it) is repainted to match, and the zoom-hint text is rewritten to describe the new scene. Hook text that names where an item sits ("on the tray's rim") is re-checked against the new art. <!-- G145 -->

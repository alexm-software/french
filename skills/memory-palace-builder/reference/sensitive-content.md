# Sensitive and mature content (merged)

Read when a room has sacred, adult, grave or mature words, or children near adult items.

- **Sacred and cultural respect**: religious content is depicted reverently. Never mock sacred figures, objects, or rites; keep whimsy in nearby props or the mnemonic character, never on or touching sacred objects; follow the tradition's own sensitivities (ask the user which ones apply, e.g. no depictions of God the Father, no animals on crosses or altars). When unsure, show the item as text instead. <!-- B17 -->
- **Audience and mature words.** Default to all ages: no crude terms, profanity or crude mnemonics (room check 7). Mature-but-necessary words are shown as text only (a museum wall plaque), never drawn. <!-- B18 B63 B149 L39 L57 -->
8. Religious and cultural imagery is reverent and follows the sensitivities recorded for the project. <!-- B64 -->
- No depiction of God the Father and nothing likely to cause religious or cultural offense. Vulgar or insulting senses carry a gentle usage warning. <!-- B150 -->
- Never place children next to alcohol or tobacco props, and nobody drinks or smokes (detail: release-gates gate 6). <!-- B169 -->
- Rooms that hold adult items (alcohol, tobacco) contain adults and animals only. Children go elsewhere. <!-- G16 -->
- **Sensitive words go to sensitive-appropriate rooms.** Death, crime, violence, illness and similar grave words never sit in a children's, farm, fun or food scene, or a light-hearted room, as a joke (no suicide verb on a "Relief Street", no killing in a hair salon). Move them to a sober wall-text room (plain wall text in a museum-style gallery) or drop the drawing. Fix these first. <!-- B221 L119 G137 -->
- **Sensitive words in new rooms.** For a grave word that lands in an ordinary room (slavery, poverty), use a blank museum-style plaque on a stand or wall, with the word only as the overlay label. Never paint the thing itself, and avoid cultural-costume shorthand for peoples or tribes. <!-- G157 -->
- **Don't over-police (user, 13:12 UTC):** harmless whimsy is fine, e.g. a bee on a Bible or cartoonish slapstick danger. Only flag the user's actual content rules (no crude content, no drinking or smoking, no depictions of God the Father, mature words as text only). Spend review effort on whether the place looks good, is cartoonish enough, and makes sense as a coherent village. <!-- G62 -->

# Representation design and placement grammar

Read before any room is painted (representation spec) and when checking sides and bands.

## Representation design (decide before painting anything)

Deciding how each kind of item is represented is part of building the palace, not an afterthought. Before any room is painted, write a short representation spec, show the user a drawn sample of each device, and get sign-off. Cover at least: <!-- L34 -->

- **Each word class** (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, function words, phrases): how it is drawn and how a learner can tell its class at a glance in a mixed room. <!-- L35 -->
- **Each grammatical feature worth remembering** (gender, verb group or irregularity, reflexive, plural quirks): which single visual cue encodes it. Give every feature its own channel (position, a costume item's color, a prop) and never reuse one channel for two features. For example, if left and right already mean gender, don't also use sides for verb group. <!-- L36 -->
- **Items that don't need a mnemonic each** (alphabet letters, number lists, article tables, sound rules): one reference prop such as a bulletin board, poster or blackboard in the beginner area, not a drawn mnemonic per letter. **Alphabet = one blackboard zoom with all letters in chalk; no per-letter mnemonics, never multiple rooms.** Each letter is a hotspot labeled "A — a" (letter — French name), with no hook (layout `noMnemonic`/`lab`). <!-- L37 -->
- **Rare or advanced words**: where they live (zooms, upper floors, an advanced area) and whether their pictures are deferred. <!-- L38 -->
- **Sensitive words**: as text on a museum wall, never drawn (builder reference/sensitive-content.md).
Check every device against the others for clashes, put the device's meaning on the word card (for example a beret icon next to the conjugation), and record the decisions in the project plan so every later room follows them. <!-- L40 -->

## Placement grammar (adapt to the language)

- **Grammatical gender**: feminine nouns clearly on the left half of the room, masculine clearly on the right (a middle zone for neuter where the language has it). Don't put a big physical divider or object splitting the room; an item near the middle may overhang the center line slightly, but its main body sits on its side. Zoom spots are their own small rooms and follow the same rule. Don't tint the walls; optionally color the word's label text by gender. <!-- L54 -->
- **Verbs**: see verbs.md.
- **Adjectives, adverbs, opposites, emotions, idioms**: place them in themed rooms where they fit, or in their own rooms when there's no natural home; opposites can face each other across a room. <!-- L56 -->
- Adjectives and adverbs sit in the 30-70 % center band, also for paint-backs (builder reference/moving-words.md); precheck.py warns outside it.

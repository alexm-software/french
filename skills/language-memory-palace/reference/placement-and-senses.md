# Placement by meaning, senses and themes (merged)

Read when assigning, moving or validating words in rooms.

- **Place by meaning, never by a shared English gloss word.** Most misfits came from this: berge (river *bank*) in the bank, éleveur (*stock*breeder) with investments, polonais (*Polish*) in the repair shop, licencier (to *fire* someone) next to embraser (to set on *fire*), cocher (*coach*man) in the stadium. "Rive" is not money because "bank" is. Run a placement check (script + teacher read) in the validator that flags any word whose only tie to its room is a shared gloss word (bank, stock, firm, coach, fire, party, star, band, scale, lead, shot, stage, league, race, game, trump, fair, parcel, shell, plain, heading, handle); each flag gets a manual check and misfits are majors. <!-- B220 L117 G135 -->
- **Gloss the sense the room teaches.** A forest teaches feuille as *leaf*, not "sheet of paper"; a sky room teaches éclair as *lightning*, not the pastry; a sacristy teaches aube as the *alb*. The gloss and picture show that sense. When one spelling has two senses in two rooms (éclair, serviette, caisse, crêpe, croissant, facteur, cascade, régime), give each sense its own word id, card and hook, so each one can't be mistaken for the other. <!-- L118 G136 -->
- Keep homonyms out of a themed room unless the room fits that sense (tronc the trunk belongs in the forest, tronc the church box does not). <!-- L113 -->
- **No overflow rooms.** Every room's words share an honest theme that can be painted as one scene. A leftover "stump", "attic" or "rack" room filled with unrelated abstract nouns can't be painted as a scene and fails: either give the room an honest abstract theme and paint it as one, or move the words to buildings whose theme fits (humanities, philosophy, problems rooms). Themed buildings get their real themed words (a sewing room teaches coudre, fil, aiguille, ourlet). <!-- L120 G138 -->
- **Phrase-only words are taught as their phrase** (en avoir marre, ne… guère, clin d'œil, lors de, afin de, au sein de), and verbs that are only ever reflexive are shown with *se*. <!-- L121 -->
- Grave or sensitive words never sit in light-hearted rooms: builder reference/sensitive-content.md.

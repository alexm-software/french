# Scope, levels, density and sectors

Read when building the inventory, levels, beginner area or sector themes.

Layer this on top of the Memory palace builder skill, which covers build order, style slider, room checks, and tooling. This adds the language-specific rules. <!-- L9 -->

## Scope and levels

- Cover the target levels (e.g. CEFR A1 to C2). Tag every word with its level so paths and quizzes can filter by level. <!-- L12 -->
- Build the inventory from frequency lists and level-based syllabi under open licenses; record sources and attributions in a credits file. <!-- L13 -->
- Level lists are noisy (everyday words can be graded C2). Cross-check level with corpus frequency and boost concrete everyday words; spot-check samples per level. <!-- L14 -->
- Keep the full word list the user asked for, rare words included, but you may defer painting the rarest words: give them a home (room or zoom) and a card with no picture until later. Leave out only obsolete words and narrow professional jargon unless the user wants them. <!-- L15 -->
- Organize by level: the core village holds beginner to intermediate words; themed advanced words go on an upper floor or back room of the same themed building; general abstract or academic advanced words may go in a temporary advanced area (e.g. a town across the bridge with a library, faculties, law court and museum), with the aim of eventually folding them into the main village. Never create numbered filler rooms; every room needs a memorable concrete theme, and a check should flag words that don't fit their room. <!-- L16 -->

## Room density

Each room's main painting holds about 10 to 12 of its commonest words, well spaced, with the verbs among them. Add zoom spots (a counter, shelf, desk or stall of about 6 words each) only where the room needs more space and the spot makes sense; there is no fixed number. Rare words can wait in a room or zoom as cards without pictures. <!-- L60 -->

- No one-object zooms: fold a zoom of fewer than about 4 words into a fuller scene; see the builder reference/planning.md.

## Beginner area (the starting point)

Greetings, farewells, polite phrases, the alphabet (on one bulletin board or poster, not a mnemonic per letter), pronunciation and sound rules, articles, numbers, pronouns, basic grammar rules, and general how-the-language-works tips. Grammar-rule rooms live here (for a village, the school). <!-- L69 -->

## Thematic sectors (adapt to the culture)

Nature (beach, mountain or ski resort, campsite, farm), university (sciences, math, engineering, computing, humanities), religious sites (churches with the user's chosen prayers in the target language, shown as blended wall or plaque text with a translation toggle and audio, plus other faiths' temples treated respectfully; follow the reverence rules in the Memory palace builder skill), home, bank, shops and market, travel (station, airport, hotel), health, government, restaurants and cafes. Pick real cultural details of the target country for authenticity. <!-- L72 -->

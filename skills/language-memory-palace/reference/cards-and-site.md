# Cards, conversations, labels, interface language, quizzes

Read when building or checking word cards, dialogues, labels, i18n or quizzes.

## Detail card for every word

Every word must have at least one natural example sentence with a translation, using the word in its main sense. Also include: definition in your own words, pronunciation (IPA plus a plain respelling) and a play button using the browser's built-in speech synthesis with a target-language voice, gender and article, plural, mnemonic explanation, false-cognate warnings, common phrases and idioms, example sentences, and level. <!-- L63 -->

## Conversations

Each room gets one or more short dialogues using its words, playable with browser speech synthesis (one voice per speaker where available), with a transcript and translation toggle. <!-- L75 -->

- Dialogues must be realistic everyday scenes (ordering at a café, booking a table, cooking with a friend, asking a waiter about the bill) with natural turns, using the room's words in context. <!-- L76 -->
- Never generate them from a template, and never have characters quiz each other ("Do you know the verb X? It means Y"). If a room has no hand-written dialogue yet, hide the conversation rather than show filler. <!-- L77 -->

## Labels

Every placed item has a label (word with article). Offer a header switch, remembered between visits, between labels always shown (default) and labels on hover or tap only. <!-- L80 -->

## Interface language

Give all site UI and meta text (guide, place names and descriptions, labels, tooltips, page title) a native/target-language toggle, keeping the strings in one i18n file. Vocabulary content (words, glosses, example pairs, mnemonics) stays as it is. <!-- L83 -->

## Quiz modes

Image to word, word to image, gender (left or right), verb group (which beret color), conjugation fill-in, listen and choose, and room recall. Filter by room, sector, or level. <!-- L86 -->

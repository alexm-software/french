# L'église — church room (fresh start)

Rebuilt from scratch in the approved ink-and-gouache storybook style matching the garage.

- Empty background first (pale cream limestone, cool slate floor, stone altar, stained glass, holy-water stoup, benches, left niche).
- Independent transparent sprites for each item.
- Feminine nouns on the left, masculine on the right.
- Sacred elements treated reverently; crow never touches the cross.

## Items

**Left (feminine)**
- la croix — crow looking up at wooden cross
- la bougie — everyday candle with pearls & tiara
- la cloche — bronze bell wearing a cloche hat
- la chaise — church chair with chessboard on the seat

**Right (masculine)**
- le calice — golden chalice on the altar
- le cierge — tall white church candle on brass stand
- l'encens — brass censer with gentle smoke
- le chapelet — wooden rosary hanging from the pew
- le banc — pink piggy bank sitting on the bench

Background and sprites generated with Grok Imagine. See PROMPTS.md for exact prompts.

## Build / preview

Follow the same workflow as the garage once the image files are placed:
- `background.jpg` (empty painting)
- `assets/*.webp` (transparent sprites)
- `room.json` (this file)

Then a small build script can composite the proof.

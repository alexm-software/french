# Art styles for the French Memory Palace

This palace uses the **ink-and-gouache storybook** style (dark ink outlines, soft natural colors, pale cream limestone, eye-level three-quarter views). Other styles are available for future palaces or rooms.

## Available styles

### 1. Ink and gouache storybook (chosen for this palace)
Dark ink contours, soft natural gouache colors, warm cream and ocher, clear silhouettes, gentle daylight.  
Example: chair with chessboard in this style.

### 2. Soft watercolor
Gentle washes, no hard outlines, pastel colors, light background.

### 3. Bold flat vector / cartoon
Clean shapes, solid bright colors, thick outlines, high contrast.

### 4. Realistic oil painting
Rich textures, natural lighting, detailed materials.

Style examples are generated with the same subject (a church chair with a chessboard) so the differences are clear.  
See `styles/` for the reference images.

**This palace is locked to ink-and-gouache storybook.**

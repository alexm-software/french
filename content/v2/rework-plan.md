# Rework plan

Design note for the church, the faiths, the fantasy verbs, the museum split, and the map. Vocabulary and doors stay. No word is dropped. Image counts below are rough.

## Church and faiths

Live now: `eglise` 16 rooms, 158 words; `concorde` 6 rooms, 61 words; `cathedrale` 2 rooms, 21 words; `presbytere` 3 rooms, 39 words.

Repair in place. Same entrances, same room list. The work is real hooks and boxes that sit on the painted objects. Sacred things stay plain and respectful: no depiction of God the Father, no animal or joke on an altar, a cross, or a holy vessel. A cognate is shown as the thing itself. A pun stays only when the prop does not touch the sacred object.

Art style: the ink-and-gouache already in each building. Do not restyle a whole building in one pass.

Pilot: `eglise/cimetiere`. The yard is already one calm scene. The only false cue is the haloed cat eating a leek for *catholique*. Replace that patch with a wooden rosary on the path, in the same box. If that edit fails twice, park the churchyard and do not repaint the other graves.

Then one room at a time: sacristy, baptistery, sanctuary, treasury, choir, saints, parish hall, and the six faiths rooms. About one image per room that fails a look, not a repaint of all 27. Rough image calls: 8 to 14.

## Fantasy verbs

Verb streets that are rows of mimes move into themed rooms that already have a real activity. A little separation stays: one themed room may hold a few verbs, not a whole street. Monsieur Verbe keeps his beret color and acts at human scale. Streets that are already real places stay.

Pilot after the church: one street segment whose verbs share one everyday scene, painted once. Rough image calls: 6 to 10 for the first wave, then stop and recount.

## Museum split

`musee` is 36 rooms and 382 words. Split the building list, not the words:

- Beaux-Arts keeps painting, sculpture, and the art rooms.
- A historical château takes the castle and history rooms. Castle content does not stay in a gallery.
- The library takes the literature rooms.
- Maison des Sens takes the senses rooms.
- Outliers move to the building whose activity already matches them.

Entrances are the existing museum doors and the sector map, retargeted. No word changes room until its painting is the one that shows it.

Pilot: one Beaux-Arts room whose boxes already sit on the art, to prove the split does not move hotspots. Rough image calls: 2 to 4 for new entrance pictures, plus 0 for rooms that only change which building they belong to.

## Map

After the split, the overview and the sector maps show Beaux-Arts, the château, the library, and Maison des Sens as separate doors. Church and faiths keep their current doors. One map painting if the sector picture must show the new doors. Rough image calls: 1 to 2.

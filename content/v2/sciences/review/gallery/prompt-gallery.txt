Everything stands on the floor. Seen from up here, people and objects are small.

A geologist is unpacking his field samples. In the middle of the floor, he leans over a low round table and tilts a desk lamp onto a sparkling mineral cluster, which throws a small rainbow.

Left half of the floor, three separate spots: toward the back, a small mine model on a low table, a rock mound with one dark tunnel mouth, rails, a lantern and an ore cart; in the middle, his wooden hand-cart with one torn burlap sack, dark earth spilling from the rip, a seedling in the tear; nearer, one plain dressed building stone, a sawn rectangular block.

Right half: toward the back, a tall purple crystal on a wooden stand with a teacup-sized mouse at its base; nearer, one rubber boot standing in a tray of dark soil, sole tread pressed into it, one worm lifting its head.

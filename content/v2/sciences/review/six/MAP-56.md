The science faculty has the low square-pier colonnade, a wooden door, the barrel vault over open glass doors, the raised right door block, the lower east wing with one window, a gravel court, and a low wall with one open gate between two piers. One low round plinth and one telescope stand in the court, clear of the wall. The flat patches are gone.

The planning plinth ellipse is centred at (1049, 451), 46×34. The painted disc is centred at (1056, 455), 53×35. That centre, not the top rim at about (1054, 446), is the ground centre. It is 7 px right and 4 px down from the plan, inside the footprint.

Outside the science area the sheet is a re-render of call 51, mean difference about 22. Lycée, dome, letters building, observatory, gear-window school, satellite-dish school, quay painters and easels, two jetties, the sailboat, and the two fenced blue sculptures are in place.

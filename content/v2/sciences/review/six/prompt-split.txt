Back center: open glass doors onto a gravel path and autumn trees. The view looks outward.

A geologist is back from the field. Paint the people and objects smaller than the room, with open floor between groups.

High on the left, clear of the stone: a wooden hand-cart with a torn sack of dark earth, soil spilling from the rip, one seedling in the tear.

Lower on the left, below the sack and not touching it: one solid rectangular stone, flat faces, no hole, no window, no door, not a kiln. A shallow pan of blue water touches the stone. A short wooden jetty stands in the water on posts, and its near end lies on the stone. It is not a table.

Back left, on a low table: one small mine model. One tunnel, one lantern, one ore cart. No wheelbarrow.

Center: a sparkling mineral cluster on its own low round table under a desk lamp, with a small rainbow.

Low on the right: one adult man sits compactly on a bench. His head is well below the crystal. He tilts one rubber boot so the sole's tread rests in a tray of dark soil. One worm lifts its head from that soil. His body, bench and tray stay together and do not reach the crystal.

High on the right, above the man's head, with empty floor between his head and the stand: a tall purple crystal on a wooden stand. One mouse touches the purple crystal itself. No cat. No other animal. The mouse is not on a stool.

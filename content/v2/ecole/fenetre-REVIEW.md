# Library garden finished-art review

Reviewer: independent pass. Painting `village/ecole/fenetre.jpg`.

The greenhouse, white picket fence, hose watering the lettuce, empty striped hammock, lavender-and-pansy bed, and cabbage bed with a terracotta pot each sit in their own box. No people and no animals. The hose's stone spout touches the left edge, and a few picket tips and a sliver of the pot rim sit outside their boxes. The arched window shows the library bookshelves, and the library click is on the tall arched window.

Le Village: boulangerie slice (v2)
- All pictures (village map, boulangerie room, counter close-up, word sprites, blank sign plaque) were AI-generated with the Grok Bot built-in image generator, then cut out and composited by script.
- The words "La boulangerie" on the sign were drawn into the sign image with the font Lilita One (Juan Montoreano, SIL Open Font License 1.1). No font files are shipped with the site.
- Glosses, memory hooks and card text were written for this project. Example sentences come from the project's own word list (data/vocab.jsonl); the éclair example was written for this slice and the choisir example is adapted from it.
- Pronunciation audio uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (French voice). No third-party code or services are used.

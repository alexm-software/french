Le Village: boulangerie (v2), café, église, mairie and école
- All pictures (village map, the five rooms, their close-ups, word sprites, blank sign plaques) were AI-generated with the Grok Bot built-in image generator, then cut out and composited by script. The alphabet letter cards on the école notice board were drawn onto the generated board by script.
- The words "La boulangerie", "Le café", "L'église", "La mairie" and "L'école" on the signs, and the letters on the école notice board, were drawn into the images with the font Lilita One (Juan Montoreano, SIL Open Font License 1.1). No font files are shipped with the site.
- Glosses, memory hooks and card text were written for this project. Example sentences come from the project's own word list (data/vocab.jsonl); the éclair, carte, serviette, croissant, orgue and carte d'identité examples, and all the école examples, were written for this project, and the choisir example is adapted from it.
- Pronunciation audio uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (French voice). No third-party code or services are used.

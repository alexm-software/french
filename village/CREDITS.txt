Le Village: boulangerie (v2) and café
- All pictures (village map, boulangerie and café rooms, both counter close-ups, word sprites, blank sign plaques) were AI-generated with the Grok Bot built-in image generator, then cut out and composited by script.
- The words "La boulangerie" and "Le café" on the signs were drawn into the sign images with the font Lilita One (Juan Montoreano, SIL Open Font License 1.1). No font files are shipped with the site.
- Glosses, memory hooks and card text were written for this project. Example sentences come from the project's own word list (data/vocab.jsonl); the éclair, carte, serviette and croissant examples were written for this project and the choisir example is adapted from it.
- Pronunciation audio uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (French voice). No third-party code or services are used.

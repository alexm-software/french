Le Village: boulangerie slice
- All pictures (village map, boulangerie room, word sprites) were AI-generated with the Grok Bot built-in image generator, then cut out and composited by script.
- Glosses, memory hooks and card text were written for this project. Example sentences come from the project's own word list (data/vocab.jsonl); one (éclair) was written for this slice.
- Pronunciation audio uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis (French voice). No third-party fonts, code or services are used.

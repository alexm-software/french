Storybook ink and gouache, eye-level inside a blue-glass covered market, daylight from the glass roof. Not one long counter. Separate stands with open floor between them, and a wide empty aisle down the middle. The near side of the stall is open onto a cobbled lane, the way back outside. Every object sits well inside the frame. No blood, no knife, no child, no letters.

Left side, three stands and one low stool, each group apart. Back stand: two red apples tied with ribbons like pom-poms, then a gap, a pile of peaches, then a gap, a pile of oranges. Middle stand: one bunch of bananas, then a gap, one pear, then a gap, a pile of strawberries. Front crate: cherries in pairs, then a gap, a bowl of olives, then a gap, a mound of almonds. A small low stand in front of those: one pie with a single drinking straw. Nothing touches its neighbor.

Right side, five separate supports. An open basket of mixed fruit. One lemon alone on a tiny wooden throne. One bunch of fresh grapes. One whole round melon. One apricot wearing a soft pale coat, on its own stool. They do not touch each other or the left stands.

No extra people, no extra food, no monkey, no sign. Blue iron and glass overhead. Cobblestones visible through the open front.

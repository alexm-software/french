Storybook ink and gouache, eye-level, cool daylight, painted to the edges. Not one long counter. Three separate tables with open floor between them, and a stairwell in the middle. Every object sits well inside the frame, with a wide band of empty wall or floor at each edge. No blood, no carcass, no child, no mime.

Left wall: one hanging sign reading BOUCHERIE, in from the left edge. Below it, a small left table holding only a plate of pink shrimp and, lower on that same table, one oyster with a pearl. A gap of empty floor separates that table from a middle island. On the island, together and touching one another: one silver-scaled fish, one seashell, and one paper kite. The fish's tail does not reach the oyster.

Center: a stairwell. Steps go down. The lower shop is visible only below, through the hole, with one person in a striped apron. Not a doorway on this floor.

Right side, three separate supports. A high shelf: a blank plaque with no letters, and under it one plate with one grilled steak. A rear table: one full-body adult in a white apron, head to feet, slicing a cooked ham, knife only on the ham. An ice crate standing alone: one whole shark, mouth shut, biting nothing. A front table: one full-body seated adult, head to feet, eating bread. The clerk, the shark crate, and the seated eater do not touch.

Exactly those three people. No extra food.

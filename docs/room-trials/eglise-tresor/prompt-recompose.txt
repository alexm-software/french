Storybook ink and gouache of a church treasury, 16:9, morning daylight, cream stone, no amber cast. The largest object is a flower-panel altarpiece on the RIGHT wall, behind a small altar table, with a restorer on a short ladder. The center of the back wall is only a plain window. Do not put the altarpiece in the center.

Exactly five mimes, each in a striped shirt, suspenders and white gloves. No sixth mime. No halos on anyone. No cat. No children in the room.

Right of the altarpiece: a red-beret mime polishes a carved oak reading stand. A small trombone stands on its own stand beside it, not touching it. A bald jeweler with a loupe holds a lidded gold cup; one toy boar sits on his bench. Front right: a bookbinder sews one big red book, and one upright dry-cell battery stands beside it. Far right: an open brown studded door onto a parish hall with people at tables, a bread-and-chalice poster, and a candle banner. The poster has no words.

Left arch: an open oak door onto a priest in a green chasuble and organ pipes, with no mime in that glimpse. Left table: a woman pats an empty gold paten, one bowling pin beside it; a nun closes a tiny gold pyx, one billiard cue beside it. Front left: a green-beret mime turns a crate into a shelf that holds exactly two books. Beside him a red-beret mime covers his mouth, and a spilled polish bottle is at his feet. A red-beret mime presents with open hands; a toy horse neighs at his knee and one golf club leans on a bench. He holds nothing.

Center: an older woman stitches wheat, grapes and a chalice in a hoop, with no halo. Behind her a small crowned wooden king has no halo. A red-beret mime gilds a brass ring on its own stand next to the king and does not touch the statue.

A stone board inside the room reads LE TRÉSOR. No other lettering.

Storybook ink and gouache, dark contours, slightly realistic and gently cartooned. 16:9 eye-level view of a cream stone vaulted church treasury on a cool morning, daylight from two tall windows, true colors, no amber cast. One shared moment: parish volunteers and five Monsieur Verbe mimes restore the vessels before Mass. Full bodies, correct hands, varied poses, not a lineup. No cat, no angel, no children in the room, no God the Father, no prophets, no crucifixion, no wounded figure, no drinking.

Left stone arch: an open oak door with iron hinges. Through it, the choir at Mass, a priest in a green chasuble at a white altar, organ pipes above. Right wall: an open heavy brown studded oak door. Through it, a bright parish hall, a poster of bread and a chalice, and a banner of candles. Clear floor in front of both doors. The only lettering is a stone board reading LE TRÉSOR over a plain iron-bound chest. That chest is not a joke.

Left table, feminine side: a woman pats an empty gold paten dry, and one wooden bowling pin stands beside the cloth, not a painted number. Beside her, a nun in a plain gray habit and white veil closes a tiny round gold pyx; exactly one billiard cue stands in a rack and does not touch the pyx.

Front left: Monsieur Verbe in a green beret, striped shirt, suspenders and white gloves, tears one crate slat loose and fits it as a shelf that already holds exactly two books.

Middle: Monsieur Verbe in a red beret, full body, covers his mouth over a spilled bottle of polish on the floor. He has not damaged a statue.

Front, left of center: Monsieur Verbe in a red beret presents the gold vessels. One golf club leans on a bench. A wooden pull-toy horse at his knee has its mouth open in a neigh.

Front center: an older woman stitches a white banner of wheat ears, purple grapes and a gold chalice, held in a brown teak hoop.

Back, just right of center: Monsieur Verbe in a red beret gilds a brass halo ring on its own stand. Beside it, not touched by his hands, a small crowned wooden king statue has no glow and no sacred monogram.

Right wall, masculine side: a restorer on a comically short ladder repaints a tall flower-panel altarpiece standing behind its own small altar table. The whole altarpiece and the restorer are on the right half.

Beside that table: Monsieur Verbe in a red beret, full body, polishes a carved oak reading stand with a cloth in both hands. A small trombone stands on its own stand a step away, touching neither the stand nor a book.

Right, left of the brown door: a bald jeweler with a loupe holds a lidded gold cup with a little cross. A toy boar sits on the bench and does not touch the cup.

Front right: a bookbinder's head and hands sew a big red-and-gold book. One upright dry-cell battery stands beside the book and does not touch it.

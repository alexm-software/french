Storybook ink and gouache, eye-level, cool daylight. An upstairs fish and deli counter: white tile, oak, ice, cream walls. Wide empty border on every edge. No hanging carcasses, no blood, no children, no animal being hurt, no mime.

Center back: wooden steps go DOWN. Through the stairwell, below this floor, a sausage counter and a striped apron. The downstairs is lower, not an arch on this floor.

Left: a small sign reading BOUCHERIE over the tile counter. A plate of pink shrimp. One open oyster with a pearl. One whole fish with large silver scales, one shell, and one small paper kite, all together.

Right: a blank plaque with no letters and no picture. One small whole shark on ice, mouth closed, biting nothing. An adult eating a piece of bread. One grilled steak on one plate. An adult in a white apron slicing a cooked ham into pale slices, knife only on the ham.

Exactly those two adults in this room, plus the apron glimpse below. No extra plates of the same food.

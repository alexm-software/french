## 7. Verb tutorial: B00_depart/verbes (next to the start), a short guided room
- Hotspots: conjugaison + parler (red beret) and être (gold beret), both moved from plan.
- Wall text only, because the verbs are live elsewhere: finir (green) and vendre (purple).
- Panels:
  1. The beret colours: red -er, green -ir, purple -re, gold irregular. A hand mirror marks a pronominal verb.
  2. Monsieur Verbe, the striped mime with white gloves, acts out every verb.
  3. Where verbs live: the ST_ streets, plus verbs inside the themed rooms.
  4. Present-tense endings, with one example per group:
     - -er (parler): je parle, tu parles, il parle, nous parlons, vous parlez, ils parlent
     - -ir (finir): je finis, tu finis, il finit, nous finissons, vous finissez, ils finissent
     - -re (vendre): je vends, tu vends, il vend, nous vendons, vous vendez, ils vendent
     - irregular (être): je suis, tu es, il est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils sont

## 8. Tracking
- **RELOCATIONS_REWORK.tsv** folds in `data/source/relocations.tsv` and adds the planned moves. Its rework status is checked against git history: was the word ever in the source room's data, and was that room repainted after the move? Every future move adds a row.
- **ADDITIONS_REWORK.tsv** lists the live rooms that gain words and the live rooms or maps that need a new painted door or map spot.
